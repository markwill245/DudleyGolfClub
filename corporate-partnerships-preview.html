Corporate Partnerships 2027

A proposed commercial partnership programme designed to create stronger relationships with businesses that want meaningful brand visibility, client and employee engagement, and a genuine connection with the local community.

Brand Exposure Business Development People & Hospitality Community Impact
Proposed Direction

More Than Sponsorship Boards

The aim is to move away from treating commercial support as a simple logo placement exercise and instead build partnerships around what matters to each organisation.

Depending on the partner, that may include brand exposure, corporate golf, client entertainment, employee benefits, event involvement, community activity, heritage projects or other agreed opportunities.

Each arrangement would be considered individually and approved by the club before any commitments are made.

Committee Safeguards

Controlled, Not Open-Ended

  • No automatic acceptance: partners remain subject to club approval.
  • No unsupported claims: audience and reach figures only used when verified.
  • No blanket exclusivity: category protection only where agreed and appropriate.
  • No unapproved pricing: commercial terms remain subject to club agreement.
Partnership Routes

A Flexible Corporate Offer

The proposed structure gives Dudley Golf Club several ways to work with larger local and regional organisations without forcing every prospect into the same package.

01

Principal Partnership

A high-level relationship combining prominent club association with selected digital, event, hospitality and community opportunities.

02

Official Partnership

A focused relationship around an agreed business category, service, event or club activity, with recognition shaped to fit the partner.

03

Community & Social Value

Designed for organisations with community, social value or ESG objectives, with activity linked to participation, wellbeing, juniors, heritage or agreed local projects.

04

Corporate Golf & Events

Corporate golf, client hospitality, staff engagement and tailored business events using the club as a relationship-building setting.

05

Competition & Event

Selected competitions or club events may offer appropriate naming, presentation, promotional or hospitality opportunities where suitable.

06

Heritage & Digital Museum

Association with the preservation and presentation of Dudley Golf Club's long history, including suitable Digital Museum and physical heritage projects.

Partner Value

Why a Business Might Say Yes

Brand exposure: appropriate presence across agreed club, digital, event and communication channels.

Business development: opportunities to engage clients, prospects and other organisations through golf and hospitality.

People: employee golf, introductory sessions, hospitality or staff engagement where agreed.

Community: visible support for a long-established local sporting institution and relevant community activity.

Club Value

What Dudley Golf Club Gains

New commercial income without relying solely on membership and green-fee revenue.

Longer-term relationships with credible local and regional organisations.

Support for agreed club, competition, community and heritage projects.

More opportunities to connect the club with the wider Dudley and Black Country business community.

Course & Tee Partnerships

Structure Currently Under Review

Course and tee sponsorship remains a potential part of the wider commercial programme. The final structure, commercial terms, sponsor rights and relationship with any external provider should be agreed before this element is offered publicly.

How It Could Work

A Simple Approval Process

A controlled process keeps the club in charge of who it works with and what each partner receives.

01

Identify

Target organisations with a credible fit and evidence of community or partnership activity.

02

Discuss

Understand what the business wants before proposing benefits or commercial terms.

03

Approve

Agree the package, rights, price and any category protection before commitment.

04

Deliver

Provide the agreed benefits professionally and review the relationship before renewal.

Committee Decision

Approve the Direction — Not Individual Deals

This preview is intended to demonstrate how Dudley Golf Club could present a professional corporate partnership programme. Individual partners, prices, contracts and commitments would remain subject to the appropriate club approval process.
